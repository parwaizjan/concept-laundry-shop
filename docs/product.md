# Product: The Concept Site

## Purpose

A polished **concept website** for a laundry shop, used as a sales tool to pitch laundry shop owners. It must demonstrate, at a glance, how a professional website helps their business win more customers — and be trivially re-skinnable into a real client site.

## Pitch value (what the demo must communicate)

- **Credibility.** A modern site makes a small laundry look trustworthy and established.
- **Convenience.** Online presence + WhatsApp ordering means customers don't need to call or visit.
- **Discovery.** Appears on Google, works on mobile, shows location and hours.
- **Professionalism.** Clear services and pricing reduce back-and-forth.
- **Reach.** English + Sinhala + Tamil so they serve more of their local market.
- **Low effort for the owner.** They keep running the shop; we handle the site.

## Audience

- **Primary buyer:** independent laundry shop owners in Sri Lanka.
- **End users of a deployed site:** local residents and businesses needing wash/fold, ironing, dry cleaning, and pickup/delivery.
- **Language:** English primary; Sinhala and Tamil optional per client.

## Page structure

Single-page concept with anchored sections, plus legal pages.

1. **Hero** — brand, tagline, primary CTA (WhatsApp), secondary CTA (view services).
2. **Trust bar** — quick stats / guarantees (e.g., 24h turnaround, free pickup over X).
3. **Services** — cards with icons, short descriptions, "from" pricing.
4. **How it works** — 3–4 steps (book on WhatsApp → we pick up → clean → delivered).
5. **Pricing** — simple table/list in LKR, per kg/item, with notes.
6. **Testimonials** — 2–4 short quotes.
7. **FAQ** — 4–6 common questions.
8. **Location / hours** — address, hours, map embed/link.
9. **Contact CTA** — prominent WhatsApp button; phone; service area.
10. **Footer** — brand, quick links, socials, legal links, language toggle.

Legal: **Privacy** and **Terms** (minimal, placeholder-friendly).

## Success criteria

- A laundry owner immediately understands the value in under 30 seconds on mobile.
- A clone can be re-branded into a distinct client site **without layout changes**.
- Lighthouse-friendly: fast, mobile-first, accessible.
- Content is editable by the agency quickly and safely.
